Darmanin says Mélenchon or Le Pen would destroy the European Community

The French justice minister backed Edouard Philippe as the pro-European candidate for the 2027 election, calling at a Barcelona forum for Europe to rearm and become a power rather than a place of discussion.

6 November 2026

French Justice Minister Gérald Darmanin warned that Jean-Luc Mélenchon or Marine Le Pen, if either wins the 2027 presidential election, would kill Europe as it exists. He said it at a forum in Barcelona on 2 November. The challenge, he said, will be for the next head of state to transform Europe into a Europe of power and not a Europe of discussion.

Darmanin said that under either of those presidents there would still be a European continent and countries talking to each other, but no European Community. He called instead for a very pro-European candidate to win the first round on 18 April 2027 and change Europe. That candidate, for him, is Edouard Philippe.

Darmanin judged the projects of the two candidates extremely clear. At the Barcelona forum he called on European states to rearm autonomously. He also called on Europe to defend itself against a Le Pen victory.

Darmanin spoke on 2 November 2026 at World in Progress Barcelona, in a conversation with Spanish Foreign Minister José Manuel Albares. French debates, he told the audience there, are often old people's debates, about pensions, health insurance and end of life. The world, he said, talks instead about quantum physics, artificial intelligence, climate and green industry. It is time, he said, to stop those debates and move towards debates of the future.

Darmanin drew one line of his own. The economic anger behind street protests deserves a hearing, he said. The far-left minority he called nihilist does not intend to change things from within governments.
