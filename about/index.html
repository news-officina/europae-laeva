About Europae Laeva

Europae Laeva follows the big stories from the European left, by one question: who pays, and who gains?

It stands for one Europe on every front: a social union, common defence, common investment and one voice in the world, paid for fairly. It is an independent publication and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any party or movement.

Articles here are drafted with AI from published sources. An editor checks most of them before they appear; an article that went out unchecked says so. Illustrations are generated with AI. Europae Laeva is independent and speaks for no party or movement.
